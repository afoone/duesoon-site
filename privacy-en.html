DueSoon Privacy Policy

Date: November 2026 · Owner: AFOONE ESPANA S.L. (afoone@hotmail.com)

Summary

DueSoon does not collect, send or sell any personal data. There is no server, no account, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you save lives only on your device.

What is stored and where

  • Your subscriptions and settings are stored in a local database on your phone.
  • Screenshot text recognition (OCR) runs entirely on the device (Apple Vision / Google ML Kit). Images never leave your phone.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled by the operating system.

Permissions

  • Notifications: only to show the reminders you configure. You can revoke it in system Settings.
  • Camera and photos: only when you choose to scan or import a screenshot.

Purchases

The DueSoon Pro one-time purchase is processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive or store payment data.

Third parties

We use no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. We share data with no one because we collect none.

Your rights

Since no personal data is processed outside your device, there is nothing to exercise. You can erase everything by uninstalling the app or via Settings → Your data → Export/Delete.
